InboxLedger reads incoming emails, pulls the invoice data out of the attachments with AI, checks the math, catches duplicates and sorts everything into booked, needs review or skipped.

FILTER

Only real documents

Emails without a PDF or image are skipped. Newsletters never reach the AI.

EXTRACT

AI reads the invoice

Google Gemini returns vendor, number, dates, line items and totals as structured data.

VERIFY

Code checks the math

Line totals, subtotal, tax and total are recalculated. A mismatch goes to review, never into the books.

DEDUPE

No double payments

A reminder with the same invoice attached is recognised by vendor and number and marked as a duplicate.

Connects to the tools a business already uses

The pipeline reads standard email files and writes a ledger. For a client it runs on a schedule against their mailbox and books into their spreadsheet or accounting tool, through an automation platform or a small script on their own account.
